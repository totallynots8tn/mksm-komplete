MKSM Komplete+ v1.3 (test build, 2026-11-03)

v1.2, plus everything played since, frozen together:

- Time Rifts 0.12.0 (experimental): rift fights in four areas. Wu Shi's co-op
  arena brings an Orochi with burning monks and priests; Mileena's secret arena

  warning, a rush, then Goro; the map 199 co-op area brings Kintaro with
  priests. And the Survival gauntlet: once Smoke's quests are done the vanilla
  way (the Pit II beaten, MK2 unlocked), his portal leads into nine of the
  game's cut Survival stages in a row, then back to Smoke for XP. A full
  gauntlet run was played on 2026-11-03.

  fatality meter refills and upgrades (the meter fills faster, and a full one
  lasts like up to 5 orbs), weapons of all 7 types brought to you in any area,
  fatality reveal, and EX moves (L2 + a special's input). Played 2026-11-03.
- Legendary Dark Knight Mode 3.11.3 (x3), Shaolin Shuffle 0.12.1 (outsider
  enemies and Mortal Kombat warriors), Environmental Kill XP 2.0.1, Unlock All
  1.1.1, Solo Co-op Paths 0.6.3.
- MKSMK Probe 0.12.8 (optional, diagnostics only). With the probe the set uses
  51 of the Recomp's 64 routine scripts.
